## Alignment with ToIP

ToIP licenses its glossary deliverables under OWFa 1.0, which grants broad
copyright permissions (reproduction, derivative works, distribution) for the
specification text. The DIDAS Glossary exercises those permissions when
transcluding or extending ToIP definitions and preserves attribution by
linking every adopted or extended term to its ToIP source. CC BY 4.0 was
chosen for DIDAS's own content because the glossary is a document, not
software: it keeps the same permissive, attribution-based spirit as OWFa 1.0
and MIT while using the standard license family for open documentation.
