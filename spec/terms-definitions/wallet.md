[[def: wallet, wallets, Wallet, portefeuille, portefeuilles, portafoglio, portafogli]]

~ The application, on a person's own device, that holds their [[ref: credential]]s and releases [[ref: claim]]s from them. The person operates it, and no copy of the credentials is kept elsewhere on their behalf.

~ In the swiyu trust [[ref: ecosystem]] the wallet is published by the Confederation and holds the e-ID alongside credentials from other [[ref: issuer]]s. It is non-custodial: the keys are on the device and the [[xref: toip-glossary, holder]] controls them.

~ The wallet is where consent is given. A [[ref: verifier]]'s request arrives at the wallet, the holder sees which claims are being asked for and the wallet releases those claims only if the holder agrees. See [[ref: selective disclosure]].

~ See also: [[xref: toip-glossary, digital-wallet]]. The ToIP Main Glossary files `wallet` as a pointer to `digital wallet` and covers custodial as well as non-custodial forms; the swiyu wallet is one specific non-custodial application.
