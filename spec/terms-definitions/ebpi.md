[[def: EBPI, Epidemiology Biostatistics and Prevention Institute, Institut für Epidemiologie Biostatistik und Prävention]]

~ The Epidemiology, Biostatistics and Prevention Institute (EBPI) of the University of Zurich — in German: Institut für Epidemiologie, Biostatistik und Prävention. A university institute working in four interconnected areas: research, research methods, services, and teaching & training, with the stated mission of improving people's health by better understanding what keeps people healthy and what makes them ill.

~ The cantonal programme *Prävention und Gesundheitsförderung Kanton Zürich* operates on behalf of the Zurich Health Directorate and is structurally affiliated with the EBPI.

~ In the DIDAS digital-health context: the EBPI is a domain actor in the health ecosystem in which credential-based use cases (such as immunisation records referencing the [[ref: International Patient Summary]] vocabulary) are discussed. Any specific role of the EBPI in a swiyu use case is defined by that use case's own documentation, not by this glossary.

~ Relation to ToIP: no equivalent term. The EBPI is an organisation rather than a concept.

~ Source: [EBPI — About us](https://www.ebpi.uzh.ch/en/aboutus.html), University of Zurich; [Prävention und Gesundheitsförderung Kanton Zürich — Das EBPI](https://www.gesundheitsfoerderung-zh.ch/ueber-uns/das-ebpi).
