[[def: ecosystem, ecosystems, Ökosystem, Ökosysteme, écosystème, ecosistema]]

~ In the digital-trust sense used throughout this glossary: a set of independent actors, technical components, [[ref: governance]] arrangements and shared [[ref: protocol]]s that interact to issue, hold, present, verify and rely on digital [[ref: credential]]s or related trust information.

~ In the general business sense: a network of organizations that enables its participants to tap into the resources, capabilities, products and services of their partners (adapted from Mark Greeven, IMD). A digital trust ecosystem is not necessarily centred on one core product or service.

~ Relation to ToIP: equivalent with Swiss extension — [[xref: toip-glossary, digital-trust-ecosystem]]. The swiyu ecosystem is a digital trust ecosystem in the ToIP sense; the broader upstream concept is [[xref: toip-glossary, digital-ecosystem]].

~ swiyu context: "the ecosystem" refers to the swiyu trust ecosystem: the totality of actors — issuers, verifiers, holders, orchestrators, [[ref: governing authorities]], service providers — that build on the [[ref: trust infrastructure]] established under the BGEID and act in one or more [[ref: trust domain]]s, interacting through common protocols under shared governance.
