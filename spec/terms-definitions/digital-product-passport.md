[[def: digital product passport, DPP, digitaler Produktpass, passeport numérique de produit, passaporto digitale del prodotto]]

~ Legal source: [Regulation (EU) 2024/1781](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32024R1781) (Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation, ESPR), Article 2 point (28): "‘digital product passport’ means a set of data specific to a product that includes the information specified in the applicable delegated act adopted pursuant to Article 4 and that is accessible via electronic means through a data carrier in accordance with Chapter III".

~ Related definitions in Article 2: (29) "‘data carrier’ means a linear barcode symbol, a two-dimensional symbol or other automatic identification data capture medium that can be read by a device"; (30) "‘unique product identifier’ means a unique string of characters for the identification of a product that also enables a web link to the digital product passport"; (31) "‘unique operator identifier’ means a unique string of characters for the identification of an actor involved in a product’s value chain"; (33) "‘unique facility identifier’ means a unique string of characters for the identification of locations or buildings involved in a product’s value chain or used by actors involved in a product’s value chain".

~ Scope of the Regulation (Article 1): "This Regulation also establishes a digital product passport, provides for the setting of mandatory green public procurement requirements and creates a framework to prevent unsold consumer products from being destroyed." It "applies to any physical goods that are placed on the market or put into service, including components and intermediate products", with exclusions listed in Article 1(2). Article 2 defines (40) "‘placing on the market’ means the first making available of a product on the Union market" and (44) "‘importer’ means any natural or legal person established in the Union that places a product from a third country on the Union market".

~ Swiss context: the ESPR is EU law. This glossary records its definition as the terminological reference; it does not assert whether or how DPP requirements apply to Swiss economic operators, nor that a DPP is issued as a [[ref: credential]] on the swiyu [[ref: trust infrastructure]].

~ Does not imply: a DPP is a product-specific data set reachable through a data carrier. It is not a [[ref: verifiable credential]], and the Regulation's definition does not prescribe a credential format.

~ Supporting definitions:

~ [CIRPASS, EU-funded project — descriptive, not a normative source]: "A structured collection of product-related data with pre-defined scope and agreed data management and access rights conveyed through a unique identifier, accessible via electronic means through a data carrier." ([CIRPASS — DPP in a nutshell](https://cirpassproject.eu/dpp-in-a-nutshell/))

~ Relation to ToIP: no equivalent term.
