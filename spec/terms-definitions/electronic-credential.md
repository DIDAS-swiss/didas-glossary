[[def: electronic credential, electronic credentials, elektronischer Nachweis, elektronische Nachweise, attestation électronique, attestations électroniques, attestato elettronico, attestati elettronici]]

~ The umbrella term of the Swiss Federal Act on Electronic Identity and Other Electronic Credentials (BGEID) for [[ref: credential]]s in electronic form. The e-ID is one electronic credential among others: the BGEID covers the e-ID **and other electronic credentials** (Art. 1 para. 1 let. c).

~ Swiss law (BGEID; see [[ref: credential]], where the articles are cited): anyone wishing to issue electronic credentials may use the [[ref: trust infrastructure]] (Art. 5 para. 1); the holder receives the credential as a data package and may keep it by technical means of their own choosing (Art. 7); on presentation the holder must be able to determine which parts of it, and which information derived from them, reach the [[ref: verifier]], with the [[ref: issuer]] learning nothing of the presentation (Art. 10). The duty of care that limits what a verifier may ask for (Art. 23) applies to the e-ID only (see [[ref: verifier]]).

~ swiyu context: in the swiyu trust infrastructure, electronic credentials are issued as [[ref: verifiable credential]]s in the SD-JWT VC format ([Swiss Profile VC](https://swiyu-admin-ch.github.io/specifications/swiss-profile-vc/)).

~ Does not imply: the legal term names a category of the BGEID. It does not by itself establish a credential's format, the issuer's authorisation for a credential type, or acceptance by a relying party.

~ Relation to ToIP: no equivalent term. This is a term of Swiss law; the nearest concept is the generic [[xref: toip-glossary, digital-credential]].

~ Relation to eIDAS/ARF: the EU framework names a corresponding concept, [[ref: electronic attestation of attributes]] (EAA); the relationship is related but not equivalent, because eIDAS 2 divides attestations into qualified, public-sector and non-qualified categories with different legal effects, while the BGEID articles cited here define no such categories. This mapping does not make eIDAS or ARF requirements applicable in the swiyu context.
