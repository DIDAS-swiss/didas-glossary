[[def: credential, credentials, Nachweis, Nachweise, attestation, attestations, attestato, attestati]]

~ A container of [[xref: toip-glossary, claim]]s describing one or more subjects. A credential is generated by the [[xref: toip-glossary, issuer]] of the credential and given to the [[xref: toip-glossary, holder]] of the credential. A credential typically includes a signature or some other means of proving its authenticity. A credential may be either a physical credential or a [[xref: toip-glossary, digital-credential]].

~ In the swiyu trust [[ref: ecosystem]], credentials are issued as [[ref: verifiable credentials]] in the **IETF SD-JWT VC** format, as profiled by the [Swiss Profile VC](https://swiyu-admin-ch.github.io/specifications/swiss-profile-vc/), The W3C Verifiable Credentials Data Model is not a supported format in the Swiss Profile; it appears below as a source of the generic definition, not as the format swiyu uses.

~ The BGEID covers the EID **and other electronic credentials** (Art. 1 para. 1 let. c), and three of its rules apply to any of them: anyone wishing to issue may use the trust infrastructure (Art. 5 para. 1); the [[xref: toip-glossary, holder]] receives the credential as a data package and may keep it by technical means of their own choosing (Art. 7); and on presentation the holder must be able to determine which parts of it, and which information derived from them, reach the [[ref: verifier]], with the [[ref: issuer]] learning nothing of the presentation (Art. 10). The duty of care that limits *what a verifier may ask for* is narrower: Art. 23 binds it only for the EID.

~ ToIP reference: [[xref: toip-glossary, credential]]

~ Supporting definitions:

~ [eSSIF-Lab]: data, representing a set of assertions (claims, statements), authored and signed by, or on behalf of, a specific party.

~ [W3C VC]: A set of one or more claims made by an issuer.

~ Language equivalents:

~ [DE] **Nachweis**, **Beglaubigung** — im BGEID-Kontext auch: **elektronischer Nachweis**

~ [FR] **attestation** — dans le contexte de la LSIE: **attestation électronique**

~ [IT] **attestato** — nel contesto della LSIE: **attestato elettronico**
