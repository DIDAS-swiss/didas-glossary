[[def: protocol, protocols, Protokoll, protocole, protocollo]]

~ An agreement that governs the procedures used to exchange information between cooperating entities.

~ Source: Oxford Reference.

~ Etymology: late Middle English, originally denoting the original [[ref: Minute of Agreement]], forming the legal authority for future dealings relating to it (Oxford Languages).

~ For the named swiyu artefact that governs authorisation in the trust [[ref: ecosystem]], see [[ref: trust protocol]] — a term of its own, not a species of this generic definition.

~ [ToIP — Trust Spanning Protocol (TSP)]: facilitates secure communication between endpoints with potentially different identifier types, using message-based exchanges. As long as these endpoints use identifiers based on public key cryptography (PKC) with a verifiable trust root, TSP ensures their messages are authentic and, if optionally chosen, confidential. Moreover, it presents various privacy protection measures against metadata-based correlation exploitations. These attributes of TSP together allow endpoints to form authentic relationships rooted in their respective verifiable identifiers (VIDs), viewing TSP messages as virtual channels for trustworthy communication.

~ ToIP reference: no equivalent term for the generic concept. ToIP defines particular protocols and the layering around them, such as [[xref: toip-glossary, trust-spanning-protocol]] and [[xref: toip-glossary, protocol-stack]].
