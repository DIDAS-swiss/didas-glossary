[[def: trust infrastructure, Vertrauensinfrastruktur, infrastructure de confiance, infrastruttura di fiducia]]

~ The federal technical and organisational infrastructure that supports [[ref: credential]] issuance, presentation and verification through common identifier, registry, status, trust and [[ref: protocol]] components. In the swiyu ecosystem these components include the Base Registry (identifiers and published keys, [Swiss Profile Anchor](https://swiyu-admin-ch.github.io/specifications/swiss-profile-anchor/)), the status registry ([[ref: status list]] publication), and the swiyu Trust Registry with its root trust anchors ([Swiss Profile Trust](https://swiyu-admin-ch.github.io/specifications/swiss-profile-trust/)).

~ Credentials themselves are issued, held, presented and verified by the ecosystem's actors: they are held in [[ref: wallet]] components, not stored centrally by the trust infrastructure. The infrastructure publishes the identifiers, keys, status information and trust information those actors rely on.

~ To be distinguished from the [[ref: trust domain]] (a governance-defined scope of common rule application built on the infrastructure) and from the [[ref: ecosystem]] (the totality of actors building on it). The trust infrastructure is the common foundation of all trust domains.

~ Relation to ToIP: no equivalent term. The nearest concept is [[xref: toip-glossary, digital-trust-utility]], a supporting service at Layer 1 of the ToIP stack, but it differs because the swiyu trust infrastructure is a specific, federally operated instance with a legal basis (BGEID), not a general utility category.
