[[def: governing authority, governing authorities, autorité de gouvernance, autorità di governance]]

~ The [[ref: governance]] body of a digital trust [[ref: ecosystem]] that is responsible for its governance framework.

~ Relation to ToIP: equivalent with Swiss extension — [[xref: toip-glossary, governing-authority]].

~ swiyu context: the competent body that makes and publishes governance decisions for a [[ref: trust domain]] via the [[ref: trust infrastructure]]. Such decisions can include: which issuers may issue which [[ref: credential]] types (issuer permissions), which verifiers may request which [[ref: claim]]s (verifier permissions), which use cases are governed and require authorisation, which claims are protected fields, which [[ref: trust statement]]s are published or withdrawn, and which requirements apply to participation in the trust domain. Under the [[ref: trust protocol]] the authority makes the governance decision and publishes, or causes the publication of, the applicable statement, using the statement type the protocol defines for it, such as `piaTS` for protected issuance authorisation and `pvaTS` for protected verification authorisation. [[ref: trust marker]]s are derived afterwards by the actors that evaluate those statements; the authority does not issue a marker.

~ Language equivalents:
