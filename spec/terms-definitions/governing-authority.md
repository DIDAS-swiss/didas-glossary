[[def: governing authority, governing authorities, autorité de gouvernance, autorità di governance]]

~ The [[ref: governance]] body of a digital trust ecosystem that is responsible for its governance framework.

~ In the swiyu context: the competent body that determines, for a [[ref: trust domain]], which actors may issue which [[ref: credential]] types, and publishes this via the [[ref: trust infrastructure]].

~ ToIP reference: [[xref: toip-glossary, governing-authority]]

~ Language equivalents:

~ [DE] **Governing Authority** (der englische Begriff wird auch im Deutschen verwendet)

~ [FR] **autorité de gouvernance**

~ [IT] **autorità di governance**
