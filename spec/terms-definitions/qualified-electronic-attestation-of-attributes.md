[[def: qualified electronic attestation of attributes, qualified electronic attestations of attributes, QEAA, QEAAs]]

~ Normative space: eIDAS 2 / EU ARF. This entry records a term of the EU framework for interoperability mapping. Nothing in it applies normatively in the swiyu context.

~ Definition in the eIDAS Regulation as amended by Regulation (EU) 2024/1183, as carried verbatim in [ARF Annex 1](https://github.com/eu-digital-identity-wallet/eudi-doc-architecture-and-reference-framework/blob/main/docs/annexes/annex-1/annex-1-definitions.md): "An electronic attestation of attributes which is issued by a qualified trust service provider and meets the requirements laid down in Annex V." It is one of the three categories the ARF groups under *attestation*; see [[ref: electronic attestation of attributes]].

~ Swiss counterpart: none established. Whether Swiss law defines a corresponding qualified category of [[ref: electronic credential]]s has not been verified against the published text of the BGEID, and this glossary asserts no such category.

~ Does not imply: recording this term does not make eIDAS or ARF requirements applicable in the swiyu context, and it does not imply that any Swiss electronic credential has the legal effects of a QEAA.

~ Relation to ToIP: no equivalent term. The nearest concept is [[xref: toip-glossary, attestation]]; the qualified status is a legal category of the Regulation that ToIP does not define.
