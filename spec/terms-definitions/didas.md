[[def: DIDAS, Digital Identity and Data Sovereignty Association]]

~ The association «Digital Identity and Data Sovereignty», DIDAS for short. A charitable, not-for-profit association under Art. 60 ff. of the Swiss Civil Code, seated in Rotkreuz in the Canton of Zug, politically and confessionally neutral.

~ Its purpose under the statutes is the establishment and promotion of Switzerland as a leading ecosystem in the development and adoption of technologies, services and products for the protection of privacy and of data sovereignty, which make digital identities together with electronically verifiable data and credentials possible, protect them and render them usable. DIDAS understands this to mean in particular authentic, machine-verifiable data and credentials that enable digital self-determination, resilience and sovereign control over one's own information.

~ The statutes name the orchestrator role: DIDAS takes on the role of ecosystem orchestrator for building, coordinating and sustainably developing a digital trust [[ref: ecosystem]] on the basis of the federal [[ref: trust infrastructure]] swiyu and beyond. In February 2026 the Federal Office of Justice designated DIDAS a swiyu Orchestrator.

~ The statutes commit DIDAS further to supporting Swiss organisations in building a multi-stakeholder ecosystem, to operating technology components including nodes in networks, to bringing together organisations of every kind from start-ups to public authorities and to working with educational institutions on curricula and research. The association is politically neutral and technically independent. It may publish expert opinions and respond to government consultations.

~ DIDAS maintains this glossary.

~ Source: Statuten des Vereins «Digital Identity and Data Sovereignty», version 2.0 of 10 April 2026, §1 to §3, on the basis of version 1.0 of 9 September 2020. Published with the regulations at [didas.swiss](https://www.didas.swiss/).

~ ToIP reference: no equivalent term. DIDAS is an organisation rather than a concept.
